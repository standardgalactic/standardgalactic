Who Oneself?

By Flyxion

[Verse 1]
I’m talking to a robot, spitting into silicon,
Wondering what the flow’s got, mic caught the car talk.
Tires in the transcript, asked for mathematics,
Got a roadside manuscript—q sub t, let the next state follow.
F of the graph, no mystical cargo,
Vertices, edges, a rule for the motion.
Don’t turn an XOR into quantum devotion,
Exclusive disjunction identifies componentwise differences,
Binary configurations, meticulous inferences.
Quantum superposition, a linear combination,
Complex amplitudes require a different explanation.

[Verse 2]
Say f of x, let the brackets stay silent,
Check what it means before making it stylish.
Alpha with the zero, beta with the one,
Modulus squared make the total sum one.
Ket isn’t a kitten and a bit isn’t a bite,
Typography’s dramatic, but the statement must be right.
I wrote the whole textbook, then requested a picture,
Now somebody’s judging the brush by its signature.
Was it human? There’s a hand on the page,
And a claim with no reason taking center stage.

[Verse 3]
Marker running dry, still the argument survives,
Twenty-five essays with the next steps inside.
You can fork it, work it, make the specification stronger,
But drawing every border makes the afternoon longer.
Who oneself? I’m talking to a robot,
Move that eye right, give the lizard hand a new spot.
Decades at the keyboard, now a sentence makes a sketch,
Ten seconds for the image, years behind the next step.
